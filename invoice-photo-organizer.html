3. How it will work

Staff sends original invoice photos → bot collects a batch → reads invoice number/date → corrects orientation and crops without generating replacement content → sorts date/number → sends Telegram albums to all enabled destinations → deletes temporary file references and processing copies.

For a private destination, open the bot in Telegram and press Start first. The bot must also be allowed to see photos in each source group. Invoice photos containing illegible numbers or dates will be marked for review rather than guessed.
