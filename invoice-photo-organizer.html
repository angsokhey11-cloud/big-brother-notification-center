3. Private forwarding and review workflow (planned)

You forward only the relevant invoice photos into your private bot conversation → bot collects that private batch → creates a private review task for every forwarded invoice → you enter or edit the printed invoice number and date for each → bot shows an editable summary → you explicitly approve the whole batch → bot carefully corrects the original photographs without recreating their content → sorts by confirmed date and number → sends albums to your mapped group/topic and private chat → deletes temporary file references and working copies.

Example private task: 📸 14 photos received. Review 1/14. The bot shows the original picture; you reply 002351 | 07/10/2026. Use Next/Previous/Edit to verify the rest, then select Approve & Send after all 14 are checked.

Privacy and safety: No correction questions in the staff group; no delivery until ALL photos are confirmed by the assigned private reviewer. Metadata is used to sort, not to alter handwriting on the image. Temporary pending reviews have a planned 24-hour expiry; the review bot will need expiry reminders and failure handling before activation.

Configure both the delivery group/topic and your private chat as enabled output destinations. Open the private bot chat and press Start before forwarding.
