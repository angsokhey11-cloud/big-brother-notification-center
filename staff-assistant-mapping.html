5. Privacy and testing

Staff types /staff in their Telegram private chat to open the live assistant. Their messages, searches, invoice photos, and Telegram snapshots are not archived by V3. Only approved mapping/language and temporary navigation IDs are kept; navigation IDs expire in 30 minutes and are cleaned every 15 minutes.

Earnings requests go to Staff Relation → Admin Work. No payout happens automatically. Existing bank-review group routing is unchanged.
